## 1. Defect series — accept the two contributions and numerical roots; reject a controlled two-term expansion

N03:116–137,145–151. In the log of the partition function one counts **connected clusters**, not just closed sheets. The one-plaquette normalization cancels exactly. An embedded orientable fundamental sheet with two faces at each link gives a factor `d^(F−E+V) u^F`: the character coefficient supplies a dimension per face, link integration an inverse dimension per edge, and a closed index loop a dimension per vertex. Both named sheets are tori. SU(2)'s fundamental is self-conjugate, not two labels; for U(1) the consistent unit flux on a connected sheet has two signs, not an independent sign per plaquette.

For the axis-aligned sheets at n ≥ 2: choose two directions and the two transverse coordinates, giving `6 n²` flats of area `n²`. For a unit square transported around a periodic axis choose that axis (4), two of the remaining axes (3), and the square's transverse basepoint (`n³`); translating along the winding axis does not give another tube. Area `4n`. A tube is null-homologous (it bounds a solid tube), but its **support** winds. Homology alone is the wrong cancellation test. Clusters that lift with identical activity and embedding density on both boxes cancel; exclude periodic self-contacts and incompatibilities before using extensivity. The term in the defect from these families is `16 S(n) − S(2n)`, where `S(n)=g(6 n² u^(n²)+12 n³ u^(4n))`, g=1 or 2. The minus sign matters; the first doubled-box powers are `4n²` and `8n`.

At fixed n the lower of the two exponents is the flat for n<4, the tube for n>4, and both tie at n=4. This is not an ordering of the *whole* series. For example on side 2 an SU(2) spin-1 flat sheet has coefficient `(I₃/I₁)^4=O(β⁸)`, the same power as the fundamental tube; replacing one plaquette of a flat sheet by the other five faces of a cube adds four faces. The log also subtracts disconnected products. Even on a doubled side 4 the two displayed doubled families tie. I did not enumerate all periodic polymers, prove minimality among general spin foams, or bound their signed remainder. A count of two embedded types is not proof of an exact next coefficient or a uniform relative error as n varies.

Independent arithmetic: evaluated `I_k(b)=Σ_{j≥0}(b/2)^(2j+k)/(j!(j+k)!)` and bisected `16 S(n)=1/7480` (no fit). SU(2), n=2, β=.4,.6,.8,1: .03741,.18359,.55696,1.29516 vs .056(18),.187(23),.591(27),1.456(31); last misses by 5.2 quoted errors. n=4, β=1,1.8,2: .0000017,.00579,.02121. The last two measurements are **increments from 1**, −.003(51), .336(63); the second discrepancy is about five errors. U(1), n=2, β=.2,.3,.4: .07532,.37257,1.14253 vs .075(7),.383(9),1.19(1); n=4 baseline at .6 estimates .0000595. Consistency at the lowest couplings, not validation of the remainder. MC comparison uses N03's tables and O03 §4 integration audit; grid error, annealing correlations and unknown absolute offsets are not included in these pulls. SU(2) model crossings **.0972014 and 1.355405** are correctly rounded in the report. Neither is a proven endpoint; in particular a measurement with .05 uncertainty cannot resolve .000134.

## 2. What a seed buys — accept the finite-box implication; qualify the contrapositive

G02:15–28; N03:110–114,139–151. Need one common rectangular `Z`, finite and nonzero, with `Z=Tr T^t` in **each** choice of time, same transverse operator when time alone doubles, ordered nonnegative spectrum with multiplicity, nonzero top eigenvalue and summable relevant powers (nonzero positive trace-class T suffices). Positivity of an arbitrary operator on one cube is not this hypothesis. For `p=2 log Z(t)−log Z(2t)` the normalized excited tail is at most `exp(p)−1`, the doubled tail at most its square; hence `p'≤2 exp(2p)p²≤4p²` for `p≤1/4`. O03 §1's commuting doublings and nonnegative edge telescope give `4(8+36+162+729)=3740`; starting at δ≤1/7480 gives `3740 δ_k≤2^(−2^k)`. N03:139 is out of date: F01 `Rectangle.lean`, especially `box_recurrence` and `conditional_gap_of_boxes` (lines 377–417), now has the rectangle induction under the edge hypotheses. I inspected the declarations, not a new strict build. It is not a formal construction of Wilson transfer operators.

With the spectral ratio bound at every scale this gives `λ₂/λ₁ ≤ exp(−log(2)/n)` on spatial sides `2n,4n,…`, at the **same β**. For rank one use ratio zero, or gap +∞, not a real logarithm at zero. The finite-volume conclusion is already incompatible with excitations whose energy tends to zero on this very sequence. To use an *infinite-volume* Coulomb theorem instead, take vacuum local states along these boxes; a bounded centered local observable has a time correlation bounded by its equal-time norm squared times `2^(−t/n)`. Pass this estimate at each fixed t to the appropriate local limit, then use the positive spectral measure. A nonzero gapless photon spectral weight in a tested observable contradicts it; for a gap of the entire reconstructed sector one needs cyclic/dense states and control of vacuum subtraction. Identify boundary conditions, sector and the limiting state with those of the masslessness theorem (e.g. the regime and action of the Fröhlich–Spencer result). A massless state in some other limit does not suffice. **No seed at any n** is a valid contrapositive with this bridge, not a consequence of recurrence alone or of two U(1) data points. G02:21 also confuses the integrand in the hundreds with the tabulated defect near 80.

For a physical bound the precise seed target is `∃L<∞ ∀β` on the chosen scaling trajectory sufficiently far toward the continuum, `∃n∈ℕ, n≥1: a(β)n≤L` and `16 log Zβ(n⁴)−log Zβ((2n)⁴)≤1/7480`. Use the **same** bare parameters on both boxes. Then the candidate rate is ≥log(2)/L in physical units. Specify whether the target is a subsequence or every tuning, and how the resulting states/normalized observables converge nontrivially. Leading-log asymptotics alone do not bound `a n`. The seed is the outstanding *estimate in this chain*, not the sole missing hypothesis of a continuum existence-and-gap theorem.

## 6. Recommended order — accept with one cheaper first theorem

1. **First seed without a cluster expansion.** With normalized Haar and the shifted Wilson weight, each plaquette contribution satisfies `|a₀|≤1`. For a box with P plaquettes, `exp(−βP)≤Z≤exp(βP)`. Since `P(n)=6n⁴`, `P(2n)=16P(n)`, it follows that `Δβ(n)≤32βP(n)=192βn⁴`. Fix e.g. n=2: every `0≤β≤1/(7480·3072)` is a sufficient seed. Extremely weak but explicit and nonzero. This proves neither the estimated .097 endpoint nor anything at weak coupling. Formalize this bound, the Wilson rectangular trace/positivity interface and the local-limit argument; these make a better first end-to-end strong-coupling lattice test than formalizing a polymer library. A convergent cluster expansion with explicit finite-size remainder is a sound *sharper* target, not necessary for the first instance.
2. **Uniform seed next.** Use the quantified target in §2. Compare the two *unnormalized* torus free energies after RG, with a common normalization and coupling tuning; volume terms cancel but an O(volume) error on each box is useless here. Bałaban-type UV stability is not an IR decay estimate. The program's `01-WHAT-OPENAI-PROVED.md` (especially lines 49–57) describes stopping at unit scale and tuning a fixed weak effective coupling; compactness/existence does not give this finite-size remainder. Check tuning is shared across volumes before borrowing any normalization. No independent audit of either full construction in this pass.
3. **Proof workflow.** Keep independent model-family challenges, primary-source checks, explicit countermodels, and Lean on frozen statements. Independence is useful for finding mistakes, not an acceptance rule. Audit translation and hypotheses separately; a kernel can verify the wrong conditional statement. Stop Monte Carlo as proof of inequalities. Keep exact small-box evaluations, certified enclosures, and preregistered falsification runs if they can rule out a proposed lemma.
4. **Topology/N02 low priority.** One matched-box comparison is reasonable only as a diagnostic. Specify group, spatial magnetic twist and flux convention, compute `−log(Z_tw/Z)` and absolute Δ at matched β,n,2n (including the integration baseline), propagate correlated uncertainties and check a soluble limit. Compare size dependence, not just correlation with β. Fourier-project weights, not free energies, if projecting sectors. No identity equates a twist cost to this untwisted defect; without a proposed inequality and a failure criterion there is no reason to extend the scan.
