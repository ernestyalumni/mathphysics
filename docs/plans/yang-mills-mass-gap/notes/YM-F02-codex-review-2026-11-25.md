**(d′) Positivity and which λ₂?** Expanding the temporal product into sign characters gives outer products of `exp(−βEs/2) χ_A`. Multiplication of temporal signs is a bijection; if their character is ever −1 its sum vanishes, otherwise the sum is |H|. The factors involving sinh and cosh are nonnegative for β≥0. This proves PSD rather than only positivity of individual entries. Positive diagonal gives positive trace. `MatrixSpectrum.lean:specSeq` uses the sorted Hermitian eigenvalues, then pads by zero; positive trace and PSD force its zeroth entry positive. At N≥1 there are at least eight spatial configurations: entry 1 is a genuine eigenvalue, not an artifact of padding. A repeated maximum would give ratio 1 and is *excluded by the result*, not by an implicit simplicity hypothesis.

The full slice is the correct space for this trace. For **this Wilson** `sliceE`, a vertex sign change cancels around every spatial plaquette; the temporal link sum absorbs a change on either boundary by a bijection of H. Thus `T=PTP` for the orthogonal gauge-average projector P. Every nonzero eigenvector lies in its range; the orthogonal complement adds only zeros. This interpretation is a paper observation about the concrete model, **not** a formal gauge-reconstruction theorem or a claim for arbitrary `Es`. No restriction to a single observable or symmetry channel. Author commit `ee8b83a` (PSD); sorted matrix interface `6db408c`.
