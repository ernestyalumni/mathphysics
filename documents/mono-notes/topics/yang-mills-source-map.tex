\section{Source map: the formal Yang--Mills construction}\label{ym:sec:source-map}
Each entry gives a labelled statement of Section~\ref{ym:sec:lattice}, the
declaration of the formal source \cite{OpenAIYM2026} that carries it, and its
file and line at commit \path{3456e5e}. Entries marked $\circ$ were located
through the table of \cite{Armstrong2026} and the declaration's signature;
all others were read at the cited line. Names in the source are not
descriptive; the mathematical reading is the one given here.
