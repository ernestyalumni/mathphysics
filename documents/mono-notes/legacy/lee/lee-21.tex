Suppose a Lie group $G$ acts smoothly, freely, and properly on a smooth manifold $M$. Show that $M$ is the total space of a smooth fiber bundle with base $M/G$, model fiber $G$, and projection equal to the quotient map $\pi\colon M\to M/G$. [Hint: see the hint for Problem 21-5.] Conclude from Problem 10-19 that $M$ is compact if and only if both $G$ and $M/G$ are compact. [Remark: any fiber bundle obtained in this way is called a \emph{principal $G$-bundle}.]
