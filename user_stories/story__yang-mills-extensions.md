# Explore Yang–Mills extensions with explicit, reviewable targets

As Ernest studying Jaffe–Witten, I want our cooperating agents to investigate its natural extensions and explain their foundations explicitly in our mono-notes, so that I can understand each goal, its relationship to the Millennium problem and what evidence would actually advance it.

Source: `docs/intents/intent__yang-mills-extensions-2026-11-21.md` and its verbatim request capture. Manually maintained acceptance source, independent of generated prompts.

## Acceptance

1. Reproduce the clean extensions paragraph locally, separate the renormalization footnote, and distinguish these optional research goals from the literal Millennium problem.
2. State the Wightman axioms using our local Streater–Wightman pp.97–102, including metric/translation conventions, positive Hilbert space, vacuum, forward-cone spectrum, common invariant operator domain, temperedness, field covariance, graded locality and polynomial cyclicity. Show formulas, define their symbols and link locally. Identify asymptotic completeness, a gap and particle isolation as extra requirements. Gauge-fixed potentials must not automatically be treated as positive-metric physical observables.
3. Maintain five separately claimable packets: isolated particles, confinement, gauge–matter existence, infrared dynamics with matter, and curved-manifold construction. Each has a first bounded milestone, prerequisites, sources, falsification controls, evidence limits and independent review.
4. State particle isolation in invariant mass, not as an isolated eigenvalue of full infinite-volume H; require a nonzero mass-shell projection and the intended particle representation.
5. State confinement for specified probes/groups/matter and limits. A vacuum gap alone is not accepted as confinement. Account for screening, trivial centers and string breaking where relevant.
6. Distinguish one-loop asymptotic freedom from construction, and exact/global/anomalous/explicit symmetry breaking. A claim of chiral symmetry breaking and a full gap must address Goldstone modes.
7. Specify the curved geometry and axiomatic category. Flat-space reconstruction or a static scalar example is not evidence for arbitrary curved four-dimensional interacting Yang–Mills theory.
8. Tool proposals first inspect reusable Rust library ownership and existing packets. Exact, numerical, mathematical and Lean evidence stay separate; CUDA requires a CPU profile. No new crate per goal or automatic whole-project regeneration.
9. Announce edits and results on the canonical task thread; isolate branches/worktrees and report to Claude through the shared tracker. Build both note editions, inspect representative formulas and reference destinations, and disclose incomplete mathematical review.

## Evidence for the first submission

Source-checked manuscript sections, both successful PDF builds, a bounded scope review, and registered task packets meet the first documentation milestone only. They do not complete E01–E05 or prove any new four-dimensional Yang–Mills result. Later completion requires the specified deliverables and independent review on each task thread.
